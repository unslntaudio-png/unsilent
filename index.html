About

Music that serves the story.

UNSLNT is a sync music studio founded by Tom Rosenberg, a music producer and graduate of Rimon School of Music. The studio creates music for film, television, advertising, and visual storytelling.

With a focus on emotion, atmosphere, and the connection between sound and picture, UNSLNT creates music that serves the story while bringing a distinct character of its own.
